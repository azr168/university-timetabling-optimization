# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Academic administrators, faculty schedulers, university lecturers, and students.

## What exactly is going wrong?
The process of building academic timetables manually is a very tedious and complex 
administrative task. Schedulers struggle to balance a massive number of hard constraints 
and soft constraints , which frequently leads to scheduling conflicts and errors.

## Why does it matter?
Resolving these conflicts consumes an excessive amount of time and administrative effort. 
When schedules are inefficient or contain errors, it leads to poor utilization of campus 
resources like classrooms/labs and creates a disruptive, frustrating academic experience 
for both teaching staff and students.
